Move WASDor ↑←↓→ Ball Spaceshoot or tackle Fpass First person WSrun, brake ADsteer QEsidestep

Three a side, first to 5. The ball stays at your feet once you reach it; run into the carrier's ball to win it. You play whichever of your outfielders has the ball or is nearest it, and pass to the teammate you are facing; your keeper looks after himself. In first person you stay as one kid and run the way you look: hold W and steer with A or D to carve a curve, Q and E sidestep, and you can click the court to look with the mouse. Esc pauses.

This game needs a keyboard.
